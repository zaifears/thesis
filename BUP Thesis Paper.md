## 4. Core Research Design (keep identical across all chapters â€” do not let wording drift)

**Dependent variable:** MFS financial inclusion/usage â€” monthly transaction volume (BDT) and/or transaction frequency

**Independent variables (compliance stringency proxies):**
- KYC burden / ease of KYC process
- Transaction limit restrictiveness
- Account freeze/block frequency
- KYC method (paper vs. e-KYC)
- Perceived monitoring intensity

**Control variables:** income, age, education, urban/rural residence, years of MFS usage, number of providers used

**Primary hypothesis (H1):** KYC/compliance friction is negatively associated with MFS transaction volume/frequency, controlling for demographic factors.
*(Note: this is a theoretically motivated hypothesis based on comparable-market literature â€” M-Pesa/Kenya, Paytm-UPI/India â€” not a predetermined result. The actual coefficient sign/significance depends entirely on real collected data.)*

**Survey instrument summary** (Google Form, bilingual EN/BN, ~19 questions, ~3â€“4 min):
- Section 0: Consent & screening (MFS user? 18+?)
- Section 1: Demographics & controls (age, gender, education, residence, income, MFS tenure, # providers)
- Section 2: Usage/DV (transaction volume, frequency, services used)
- Section 3: KYC & compliance experience/IVs (KYC method, ease, limit restriction frequency, freeze history, monitoring perception, satisfaction)
